reset attempts. No source re-authoring or new source-defect policy is introduced.

Controller-owned diagnostic reports and accepted-debt metadata remain exact,
authenticated read-only evidence, not provider-editable identity definitions.
Missing, changed or tampered inputs cannot be papered over by recapturing them.
The source baseline and all predecessor completion proofs remain authenticated.

## Execution and recovery

Bind the initial derivation to the exact released WHY2 or accepted-debt successor;
bind later derivations to their actual failed gate predecessor. Retain every
operation and review round. Only the existing publisher may expose returned
derived bytes, deterministic reports and regenerated graph outputs. Completion
proofs bind native routing/effects and are replayed without provider calls,
duplicate charges or repeated gate attempts.

Gate-disabled routing goes directly to the existing Phase 1 checkpoint. A passed
gate reaches that checkpoint; failed/pending/no-progress/exhausted outcomes use
the existing native policy. The checkpoint must authenticate current quality or
debt authority plus the required Lexicon evidence before preparing its native
decision. Approval ends this milestone at Phase 2's entry; rejection preserves
the native stopped outcome. Neither outcome executes a Phase 2 producer.
