# Overview — Spec 015: Cognitive Architecture Outcomes Validation

**Spec ID**: 015 | **Run**: squad-1775154996 | **Phase**: WHAT | **Date**: 2026-04-02

Spec 015 answers the question "can you prove this outcomes?" raised after spec 014 (Cognitive Architecture Mechanisms for Echelon) was delivered. It does so by requiring a systematic, citation-backed verdict on every one of spec 014's 17 claimed outcomes, organized into a Proof Topology Table that assigns each claim to one of five proof categories: P1 (Proven by Paper), P2 (Proven by Design), P3 (Requires Prototype), P4 (Gate-Conditioned), or P5 (Speculation). The two NS-003 components — Generator-Critic schema compliance and AGM belief revision contradiction catching — carry Grade A paper evidence from NL2GenSym (arxiv:2510.09355) and Kumiho (arxiv:2603.17244) respectively, making them the only claims that are proven at the component level; their Echelon-specific validation still requires a prototype. All five CA overlay claims (Goal Stack, ACT-R Typed Buffer, LIDA Broadcast, GWT Bounded Workspace, Episodic Memory) are explicitly gate-conditioned on the U-CA-004 three-condition experiment not yet run, and no overlay may be stated as proven or ready for implementation until that gate resolves positively. The 40-70% token reduction claim for NOVEL-004 is explicitly labeled SPECULATION throughout and cannot be upgraded without N=50+ prototype measurements. Beyond the verdict table, the spec requires three baseline measurements (token efficiency, scope violation rate, contradiction rate) from existing Echelon spec run artifacts — all resolvable within days using the available corpus of runs 008-014 — plus complete, third-party-executable experiment designs for NS-003 prototype validation and the U-CA-004 gate experiment. The spec produces no implementation, no roadmap, and no architecture decision; its sole deliverable is a defensible, reproducible evidence record that answers the proof question for each claim.
