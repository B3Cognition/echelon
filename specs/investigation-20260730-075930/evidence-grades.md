# Evidence Grades — SUE Second-Thoughts Review

Checked 2026-07-30. Grades follow `extension/templates/evidence-grades.md`: A = peer-reviewed research, standard, or high-integrity direct measurement; B = official documentation or reproducible benchmark; C = reputable but not independently validated; D = anecdotal/single-run evidence; E = unverified secondary material or model-generated synthesis. Patent publications are graded B only as evidence that a disclosure exists, never as evidence of validity, infringement, patentability, or freedom to operate.
