| K1 | Are SUE’s extracted typed edges and source spans correct relative to an independently annotated interpretation? | Blocks every inference from graph agreement/disagreement to specification quality. | High: systematic extraction error can be promoted as “real fracture.” | Frozen two-annotator gold audit with adjudication; measure edge precision/recall/F1, provenance accuracy, and inter-annotator agreement. |
| K2 | Can the exact inputs and raw outputs for both passes of the checked-in self-run be reconstructed? | Determines whether existing data can support a no-call retrospective audit. | Medium-high: if unavailable, the cheapest validation opportunity is lost and the artifact is not reproducible. | Audit git objects, debug artifacts, prompt/schema/tool versions, model tags, and hashes. | K3 | How much variance comes from stochastic sampling, framing, vocabulary normalization, provider/model, alignment, and source-spec differences? | Needed before defining a genuine noise-relative comparison or sample size. | High: a single blended “floor” can hide bias and misclassify fractures. | Crossed pilot with multiple specs, ≥3 passes, locked conditions, and a preregistered variance-components analysis. |
| K4 | What human behavior or downstream artifact is the gold standard for “material semantic incompatibility”? | Determines whether SUE predicts anything that matters. | High: agreement can improve while implementation decisions remain wrong. | Predefine decision tasks (test obligation, state transition, API behavior), independent adjudication, and explicit costs of false positives/negatives. |
| K5 | Are the five proposed mutation operators realistic, non-equivalent, independently seeded, and detectable through the intended channel? | Determines whether A2/A3 measure useful sensitivity or mutation artifacts. | Medium-high: easy or leaked mutants inflate performance. | Expert-approved mutant protocol, clean-site controls, operator-specific reporting, and hidden source-spec-grouped evaluation. |
| K6 | What clean-spec prevalence and independence assumptions support the false-positive target? | Determines sample size and whether A4 can be claimed. | High for a workflow gate. | At least 29 independent clean specs for a zero-FP one-sided 95% bound below 10%; increase or model clustering if any FP/correlation occurs. |
| K7 | What effect size and outcome prevalence justify ΔAUC ≥0.05 and Cohen’s d ≥0.8? | Determines power, practical significance, and whether A5 is meaningful. | Medium-high: arbitrary thresholds create false success/failure. | Pilot-based power analysis; paired/clustered effect estimates with confidence intervals; predefine minimum decision utility. |
| K8 | Does clarification-question ranking improve decisions compared with direct reading, self-reflection, deterministic smells, ClarifyGPT/AskBench-style policies, and formal baselines at equal budget? | Determines whether question utility adds value. | High: questions may consume time without reducing costly uncertainty. | Budget-matched ablation/baseline study with human answerability, redundancy, and downstream decision gain. |
| K9 | Does bounded dialectic improve error detection after cold aggregation, or merely add conformity and cost? | Determines whether dialectic belongs beyond a research ablation. | Medium: avoidable model calls and confident consensus can harm evidence quality. | Equal-budget comparison of no dialogue, structured one-round critique, and bounded dialogue; preserve pre-dialogue answers. |
| K10 | Which ambiguity types in real projects correlate with rework, defects, or decision reversals? | Determines external validity and threshold calibration. | High for claims of practical value. | Retrospective, multi-project dataset with source-spec-grouped validation and carefully defined, non-causal outcome labels. |

| K12 | Does any publication or claim set anticipate the exact SUE combination? | Relevant only if an IP strategy is contemplated, not to technical validation. | Potentially high for IP, irrelevant to immediate research validity. | Professional, jurisdiction-specific patent and non-patent literature search with claim charts and counsel. | K13 | How should historical PDFs be versioned and cited after their claims are superseded? | Affects future provenance and prevents model-generated synthesis from becoming accidental authority. | Medium. | Add provenance/status metadata and link each surviving claim to independently graded primary evidence. |
| K14 | What call, token, latency, and human-annotation budget is acceptable for the pilot and later evaluation? | Determines feasibility but not epistemic validity. | Medium. | Treat A6 as a separate product SLO; record model and human costs independently. |
