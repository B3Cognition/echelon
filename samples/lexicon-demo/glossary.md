# Glossary — Overdue invoice reminders

- **Billing_Service**: the service that issues invoices and overdue reminders
- **invoice**: a billing record owed by a customer
- **due_date**: the date an invoice payment is due
- **overdue_invoice_email**: the reminder email sent for an overdue invoice
- **delivery_time**: seconds elapsed from trigger to mail-relay acceptance
- **email_status**: the lifecycle state of a reminder email
- **RELAY_REJECTED**: error code emitted when the mail relay rejects a message
